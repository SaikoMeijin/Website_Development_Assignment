What I'm building

Sarvshala

SPUV Group Private Limited · Executive Director and COO · Hyderabad · Nov 2026 – present

Sarvshala is a school management system with an ERP portal for billing, homework and attendance. It also has transport safety: RFID ID cards and live bus GPS tell guardians each step of a child's day.

  • The idea was mine. I met 20 schools and institutions in Hyderabad with our CEO to test it before we built anything.
  • Cut the cost of each RFID reader from ₹15,000 to ₹3,000 by sourcing from manufacturers in China through Alibaba, after Indian quotes left us no profit.
  • Drafted the agreement with the startup building our production website, and negotiated its price with our CEO.
  • Handle the company's paperwork with our CA, including registering SPUV Group in November 2026.
